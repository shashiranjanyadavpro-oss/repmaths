# RepMaths Fitness Calculator Suite

RepMaths is a free fitness calculator website providing practical calculators for strength training, gym programming, nutrition, body composition, and equipment loading.

## Strength calculators

- [1RM Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/strength/1rm-calculator) — Estimate your one-rep max from any weight and rep count.
- [Bench Press Max Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/strength/bench-press-1rm-calculator) — Estimate your max bench press from weight and reps.
- [Squat Max Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/strength/squat-1rm-calculator) — Estimate your max squat from weight and reps.
- [Deadlift Max Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/strength/deadlift-1rm-calculator) — Estimate your max deadlift from weight and reps.
- [Overhead Press Max Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/strength/overhead-press-1rm-calculator) — Estimate your strict press or military press max from weight and reps.
- [Rep Max Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/strength/rep-max-calculator) — Convert a known weight and reps to any target rep count.
- [1RM Percentage Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/strength/1rm-percentage-calculator) — Find your weight at any training percentage or reverse-calculate your 1RM.
- [Strength Standards Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/strength/strength-standards-calculator) — Compare your bench, squat, deadlift, or OHP 1RM with bodyweight-based reference standards.

## Training calculators

- [Training Max Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/training/training-max-calculator) — Convert your 1RM into a training max for 5/3/1 programs.
- [5/3/1 Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/training/531-calculator) — Generate the full Wendler 5/3/1 four-week cycle from your training max.
- [Warm-Up Set Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/training/warmup-set-calculator) — Generate your full warm-up ramp from the empty bar to your working weight.
- [RPE & RIR Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/training/rpe-rir-calculator) — Convert a completed set into an estimated 1RM using the RTS RPE chart.

## Equipment calculators

- [Barbell Plate Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/equipment/plate-calculator) — Find which plates to load on each side of the bar.

## Nutrition calculators

- [TDEE Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/nutrition/tdee-calculator) — Find your daily maintenance calories using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation.
- [BMR Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/nutrition/bmr-calculator) — Find your Basal Metabolic Rate using Mifflin-St Jeor or Harris-Benedict.
- [Macro Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/nutrition/macro-calculator) — Convert your daily calories into protein, carbohydrate, and fat targets.
- [Protein Intake Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/nutrition/protein-calculator) — Find your daily protein range based on sports nutrition guidance.
- [Water Intake Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/nutrition/water-intake-calculator) — Find a practical daily water target based on body weight and activity level.
- [Calorie Deficit Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/nutrition/calorie-deficit-calculator) — Calculate your daily calorie deficit, weight-loss target, and estimated timeline.
- [Body Fat Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/nutrition/body-fat-calculator) — Estimate body fat percentage using the US Navy tape-measure method.
- [BMI Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/nutrition/bmi-calculator) — Calculate adult BMI and see your BMI category and BMI-based weight range.
- [Lean Body Mass Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/nutrition/lean-body-mass-calculator) — Calculate lean body mass from body fat percentage or predictive equations.
- [FFMI Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/nutrition/ffmi-calculator) — Calculate fat-free mass index and normalized FFMI from body composition.
- [Ideal Weight Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/nutrition/ideal-weight-calculator) — Compare ideal body weight estimates with a BMI-based healthy weight range.
- [Calories Burned Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/nutrition/calories-burned-calculator) — Estimate calories burned from walking, running, lifting, cycling, cardio, and other activities using MET values.

## How RepMaths calculators work

RepMaths calculators are browser-based fitness and nutrition tools. Each calculator uses the formulas, assumptions, reference ranges, or methodology described on its individual page.

Results are estimates where the underlying method is predictive or reference-based. Users should review the methodology and limitations provided on the relevant calculator page.

RepMaths calculators are informational fitness and nutrition tools. They are not substitutes for individualized medical diagnosis, treatment, or professional advice.
