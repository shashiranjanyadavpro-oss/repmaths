# RepMaths Fitness Calculator Suite

> RepMaths is a fitness calculator suite with 25 tools covering strength training, gym programming, nutrition, body composition, and equipment loading. All calculators run entirely in the browser.

## How RepMaths is organized

RepMaths calculators are organized into four categories:

- [Strength calculators](https://repmaths.com/tools/strength/) — 1RM, bench press, squat, deadlift, overhead press, rep max, percentages, strength standards
- [Training calculators](https://repmaths.com/tools/training/) — 5/3/1, training max, RPE/RIR, warm-up sets
- [Equipment calculators](https://repmaths.com/tools/equipment/) — barbell plate loading
- [Nutrition calculators](https://repmaths.com/tools/nutrition/) — TDEE, BMR, macros, protein, water intake, calorie deficit, body fat, BMI, lean body mass, FFMI, ideal weight, calories burned

Full calculator directory: [All tools](https://repmaths.com/tools/)

## Strength calculators

- [1RM Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/strength/1rm-calculator/) — Calculate one-rep max using Epley, Brzycki, Lander, and Lombardi formulas. Estimate your one-rep max in pounds (lbs) or kilograms (kg) for squat, bench press, deadlift, and other lifts.
- [Bench Press Max Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/strength/bench-press-1rm-calculator/) — Estimate your bench press max from weight and reps using Epley, Brzycki, Lander, or Lombardi formulas in lb or kg.
- [Squat Max Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/strength/squat-1rm-calculator/) — Estimate your back squat 1RM using Epley, Brzycki, Lander, and Lombardi formulas.
- [Deadlift Max Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/strength/deadlift-1rm-calculator/) — Estimate your deadlift 1RM using Epley, Brzycki, Lander, and Lombardi formulas without directly testing a true 1RM.
- [Overhead Press 1RM Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/strength/overhead-press-1rm-calculator/) — Estimate your strict press one-rep max using Epley, Brzycki, Lander, or Lombardi formulas from a submaximal set.
- [Rep Max Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/strength/rep-max-calculator/) — Convert a known weight and rep count to any target rep range using the Epley, Brzycki, Lander, or Lombardi formula.
- [1RM Percentage Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/strength/1rm-percentage-calculator/) — Calculate 50%, 60%, 70%, 80%, 90%, 95% and other percentages of your one-rep max in lb or kg, or reverse-calculate your 1RM from a known training weight.
- [Strength Standards Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/strength/strength-standards-calculator/) — Compare your 1RM for bench press, squat, deadlift, and overhead press with bodyweight-based reference standards for male and female lifters in lb or kg.

## Training calculators

- [Training Max Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/training/training-max-calculator/) — Convert your 1RM to an 85%, 90%, or 95% training max for 5/3/1 and Wendler programs, rounded to the nearest 5 lb or 2.5 kg.
- [5/3/1 Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/training/531-calculator/) — Generate all 4 weeks of Wendler 5/3/1 working weights from your training max, including AMRAP sets, deload, and Boring But Big assistance work.
- [Warm-Up Set Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/training/warmup-set-calculator/) — Generate a barbell warm-up ramp from the empty bar to your first working set, with reps and rest guidance.
- [RPE & RIR Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/training/rpe-rir-calculator/) — Convert a completed set into an estimated one-rep max using the RTS RPE chart, and see your weight at any target RPE.

## Equipment calculators

- [Barbell Plate Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/equipment/plate-calculator/) — Find the plates per side for any target weight in pounds or kilograms. Supports Olympic bars and plate milestones.

## Nutrition calculators

- [TDEE Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/nutrition/tdee-calculator/) — Estimate maintenance calories using the Mifflin-St Jeor formula, with BMR, cutting and bulking targets, lb/kg input, and activity levels.
- [BMR Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/nutrition/bmr-calculator/) — Calculate basal metabolic rate using Mifflin-St Jeor or Harris-Benedict in lb, ft/in, or kg, cm. Compares BMR, TDEE, and RMR.
- [Macro Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/nutrition/macro-calculator/) — Convert daily calories into protein, carb, and fat targets. Supports cutting, maintenance, bulking, keto, or custom ratios.
- [Protein Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/nutrition/protein-calculator/) — Estimate daily protein intake in grams by body weight (lb or kg) for muscle gain, cutting, and activity.
- [Water Intake Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/nutrition/water-intake-calculator/) — Estimate daily fluid intake by body weight, activity, and hot weather, in liters, US fl oz, or cups.
- [Calorie Deficit Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/nutrition/calorie-deficit-calculator/) — Estimate BMR, TDEE, daily calorie target, weekly loss rate, and time to reach a goal weight.
- [Body Fat Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/nutrition/body-fat-calculator/) — Estimate body fat percentage using the US Navy tape measure method or a BMI estimate. Enter neck, waist, height, and optional weight in inches or cm.
- [BMI Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/nutrition/bmi-calculator/) — Calculate BMI in pounds and inches or kilograms and centimeters. Shows adult BMI category, healthy BMI range, and estimated weight range for adults 20 and older.
- [Lean Body Mass Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/nutrition/lean-body-mass-calculator/) — Calculate lean body mass (LBM) in lb or kg from body fat percentage, or estimate it using the Boer, James, or Hume formulas.
- [FFMI Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/nutrition/ffmi-calculator/) — Calculate raw and normalized Fat-Free Mass Index from weight, height, and body fat. Supports lb and kg.
- [Ideal Weight Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/nutrition/ideal-weight-calculator/) — Compare ideal body weight estimates from Devine, Robinson, Miller, and Hamwi formulas with a healthy BMI weight range.
- [Calories Burned Calculator](https://repmaths.com/tools/nutrition/calories-burned-calculator/) — Estimate calories burned from walking, running, cycling, and weightlifting using MET values, in lb or kg.

## Formulas used

- **1RM estimation:** Epley, Brzycki, Lander, Lombardi
- **BMR/TDEE:** Mifflin-St Jeor, Harris-Benedict
- **Body fat:** US Navy tape-measure method
- **RPE/RIR:** Reactive Training Systems (RTS) chart
- **Protein intake:** ISSN sports nutrition guidance
- **Calories burned:** MET values
- **Ideal body weight:** Devine, Robinson, Miller, Hamwi

## How RepMaths calculators work

RepMaths calculators are browser-based fitness and nutrition tools. Each calculator uses the formulas, assumptions, reference ranges, or methodology described on its individual page.

Results are estimates where the underlying method is predictive or reference-based. Users should review the methodology and limitations provided on the relevant calculator page.

## Important

RepMaths calculators are informational fitness and nutrition tools. They are not substitutes for individualized medical diagnosis, treatment, or professional advice.
