# RepMaths 

RepMaths is a free collection of fitness calculators for strength training, workout planning, barbell plate loading, nutrition, and body composition. The calculators run in the browser.

Website: https://repmaths.com

## Calculator categories

- **Strength:** 1RM, bench press, squat, deadlift, overhead press, rep max, 1RM percentage, strength standards
- **Training:** training max, 5/3/1, warm-up sets, RPE and RIR
- **Equipment:** barbell plate calculator
- **Nutrition and body composition:** TDEE, BMR, macros, protein, water intake, calorie deficit, body fat, BMI, lean body mass, FFMI, ideal weight, calories burned

Full list with descriptions: https://repmaths.com/tools/

## Methods

- 1RM: Epley, Brzycki, Lander, and Lombardi formulas
- BMR and TDEE: Mifflin-St Jeor and Harris-Benedict equations
- Body fat: US Navy tape-measure method
- RPE and RIR: Reactive Training Systems (RTS) chart
- Protein: ISSN sports nutrition guidance
- Calories burned: MET values

## Disclaimer

Results are estimates for general fitness planning and education. They are not medical advice. Consult a qualified professional before making health or training decisions.

## Technology

Built with Astro as a static site and hosted on Cloudflare Pages.
